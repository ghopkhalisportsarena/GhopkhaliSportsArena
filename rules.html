GHOPKHALI SPORTS ARENA

Rules & Regulations.

Official club rules, discipline guidelines, sportsmanship standards and responsibilities for all members and participants.

GSA

Club Discipline & Sportsmanship

Ghopkhali Sports Arena is committed to maintaining a respectful, disciplined and positive sporting environment for everyone.

01

Discipline & Respect

Every member must maintain discipline, respect and sportsmanship during all club activities.

02

Respect for Others

All players must respect teammates, opponents, officials, organisers and other members of the sports community.

03

Prohibited Behaviour

No abusive, violent, discriminatory or harmful behaviour will be tolerated during club activities or events.

04

Club Property & Equipment

Club property, sports equipment and facilities must be used responsibly and carefully.

05

Financial Responsibility

Club funds must be managed and spent responsibly. Unnecessary or unauthorised expenditure is not permitted.

06

Official Instructions

All members must follow official instructions issued by authorised Ghopkhali Sports Arena representatives.

07

Match & Tournament Decisions

All tournament and match participants must follow the decisions of organisers, referees and authorised match officials.

08

Club Reputation

Every member must protect the dignity, reputation and interests of Ghopkhali Sports Arena.

09

Disciplinary Action

The club reserves the right to take appropriate disciplinary action when necessary for violations of club rules.

10

Fair Play

All players and members are expected to demonstrate honesty, fairness, teamwork and good sportsmanship.

11

Event Participation

Participants must follow the registration, schedule, venue and participation requirements announced for each event.

12

Club Decisions

Official decisions made by authorised club representatives regarding club activities must be respected.

MEMBER RESPONSIBILITIES

Play fair. Represent well.

01

Respect

Treat teammates, opponents, officials and organisers with respect.

02

Discipline

Maintain proper behaviour during every club activity and sporting event.

03

Teamwork

Support teammates and contribute positively to the club community.

04

Fair Play

Compete honestly and uphold the spirit of sportsmanship.

!
Important Notice

These rules are intended to maintain a safe, respectful, organised and sportsmanlike environment within Ghopkhali Sports Arena. The club may update or modify its rules when necessary.
