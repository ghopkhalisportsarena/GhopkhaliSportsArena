02 / ABOUT

MORE THAN A CLUB

A place to play, belong & grow.

Ghopkhali Sports Arena, Sports Club is a community-focused platform for athletes, sports enthusiasts and people who simply love staying active.

We believe sport creates stronger individuals, stronger friendships and a stronger community.

Our mission is simple: create an organised, respectful and energetic sporting environment where everyone can participate, compete and grow.

OFFICIAL CLUB POLICY

Rules & Regulations

01. Respect

All players, members, officials and visitors must be treated with respect.

02. Fair Play

Players must maintain sportsmanship and fair competition at all times.

03. Discipline

Every participant must follow the instructions of club officials and event organisers.

04. Club Property

Club equipment and facilities must be used responsibly.

05. Financial Discipline

Club funds and resources must only be used for approved sporting and organisational purposes.

06. Match Conduct

Teams participating in friendly or tournament matches must comply with the agreed match conditions.

07. Safety

Participants must prioritise safety during all sporting activities.

08. Committee Authority

The club committee reserves the right to take appropriate action when rules are violated.
