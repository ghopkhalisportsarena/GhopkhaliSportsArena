01 / ABOUT

MORE THAN A CLUB

A place to play, belong & grow.

Ghopkhali Sports Arena, Sports Club is a community-focused platform for athletes, sports enthusiasts and people who simply love staying active.

We believe sport creates stronger individuals, stronger friendships and a stronger community.

Our mission is simple: create an organised, respectful and energetic sporting environment where everyone can participate, compete and grow.
